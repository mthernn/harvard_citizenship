Methodology

วิธีการของเรา

We combine one-on-one tutoring, multilingual matching, weekly accountability, and legal referral pathways when specialized expertise is needed.

Welcome meeting

Program directors contact each registrant within approximately two days to understand goals, language needs, and availability.

Match

Students are matched with trained Harvard College tutors based on language, schedule, tutoring format, and citizenship stage.

Weekly support

Tutors meet at least once a week online or in person, with more frequent sessions when needed.

Naturalization journey

Students receive support through preparation, application milestones, interview practice, and the final ceremony.

Confidentiality

ความเป็นส่วนตัวมาก่อน

The program collects only the information needed to contact students, understand language preferences, and match students with tutors. Student information is stored in official HKS-IOP program files and is accessible only to authorized HKS-IOP personnel and program directors. We do not share information with partners or outside organizations unless the student explicitly requests referral support. We never publish a student’s name, photo, story, or personal information without written permission.

The Harvard Citizenship Program does not provide legal advice or legal representation. When legal questions arise, directors connect students with trusted legal partners and Harvard Law School-affiliated support pathways when appropriate.

FAQ

คำถามที่พบบ่อย

Is the program really free?

Yes. Every service provided by the Harvard Citizenship Program is free. Students do not pay for tutoring, ESL support, civics preparation, N-400 support, interview practice, or program participation.

Who can receive support?

Any adult residing in the Commonwealth of Massachusetts may register. Students do not need to be Harvard employees or already connected to Harvard.

Can I register for someone else?

Yes. Family members, Harvard employees, and community partners may register an adult learner. We will follow up with the student directly when appropriate.

Can I receive help if I do not speak English well?

Yes. We provide ESL support and try to match students with tutors who speak their preferred language whenever possible.

Can I request a tutor who speaks my language?

Yes. Tutor language capacity includes Spanish, Mandarin, Haitian Creole, French, Cantonese, Vietnamese, Arabic, Russian, Italian, Hindi, Cape Verdean Creole, Korean, German, Telugu, Amharic, Tagalog, Japanese, Urdu, Albanian, Farsi, Thai, Punjabi, Yoruba, Swahili, Mongolian, and English.

Can you help with the N-400?

Yes. Trained tutors can help students understand and work through the N-400 process. Students remain responsible for their own application.

Does the program provide legal advice?

No. The Harvard Citizenship Program does not provide legal advice or legal representation. When legal questions arise, directors connect students with trusted legal partners when appropriate.

How often will I meet with a tutor?

Students meet with tutors at least once per week. Additional meetings may be arranged depending on the student’s needs and tutor availability.

Can tutoring be online or in person?

Yes. Tutoring may be virtual or in person, arranged between the student and tutor. In-person meetings may take place at community centers, libraries, or Harvard buildings.

When will I be matched?

The program has three match windows. Registrations from July 1 to September 30 join the September cohort. Registrations from October 1 to February 28 join the February cohort. Registrations from March 1 to June 30 join the June cohort.

Is my information private?

Yes. Confidentiality is central to the program. We do not share student information with partners or outside organizations unless the student explicitly requests referral support.

Will my photo or story be shared?

Never without written permission.
