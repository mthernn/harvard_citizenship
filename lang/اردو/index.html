Methodology

ہمارا طریقہ

We combine individualized tutoring, multilingual matching, weekly accountability, and legal referral pathways when specialized expertise is needed.

Welcome meeting

Program directors contact each registrant within approximately two days to understand goals, language needs, and preferred tutoring format.

Match

Students are matched with trained Harvard College tutors based on language, tutoring format, and citizenship stage.

Weekly support

Tutors meet at least once a week online or in person, with more frequent sessions when needed.

Naturalization journey

Students receive support through preparation, application milestones, interview practice, and the final ceremony.

Confidentiality

رازداری پہلے

The program collects only the information needed to contact students, understand language preferences, and match students with tutors. Student information is stored in official HKS-IOP program files and is accessible only to authorized HKS-IOP personnel and program directors. We do not share information with partners or outside organizations unless the student explicitly requests referral support. We never publish a student’s name, photo, story, or personal information without written permission.

The Harvard Citizenship Program does not provide legal advice or legal representation. When legal questions arise, directors connect students with trusted legal partners and Harvard Law School-affiliated support pathways when appropriate.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the program really free?

Yes. Every service provided by the Harvard Citizenship Program is free. Students do not pay for tutoring, ESL support, civics preparation, N-400 support, interview practice, or program support.

Who can receive support?

Any adult residing in Massachusetts may register. Students do not need to be Harvard employees or Harvard-affiliated.

Can I register for someone else?

Yes. A family member, Harvard employee, community organization, public office, school, library, church, or other formal organization may help someone register.

Can I receive help if I do not speak English well?

Yes. We provide ESL support and try to match students with tutors who speak their preferred language whenever possible.

Can I request a tutor who speaks my language?

Yes. Tutor language capacity includes Spanish, Mandarin, Haitian Creole, French, Cantonese, Vietnamese, Arabic, Russian, Italian, Hindi, Cape Verdean Creole, Korean, German, Telugu, Amharic, Tagalog, Japanese, Urdu, Albanian, Farsi, Thai, Punjabi, Yoruba, Swahili, Mongolian, and English.

Can you help me with the N-400?

Yes. Trained tutors can help students understand and work through the N-400 process. Students remain responsible for their own applications.

Can the program give legal advice?

No. The Harvard Citizenship Program does not provide legal advice or legal representation. When legal questions arise, directors connect students with trusted legal partners when appropriate.

How often will I meet with a tutor?

Students meet with tutors at least once per week. Additional meetings may be arranged depending on student needs and tutor capacity.

What happens if I miss a session?

We understand that students have work, family, health, and transportation responsibilities. Excused absences are allowed. Regular communication and attendance are required, especially for students receiving filing-cost support.

Is my information private?

Yes. Confidentiality is a core responsibility of the program. We do not share student information, photos, or stories without permission.
